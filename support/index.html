Support

ATENVA helps you take an attention pause and choose one useful next step toward your Goals.

Do I need Screen Time permission?

No. After completing local setup, you can use Take an Attention Pause, Goals, Useful Actions, Saved Content, and Weekly Review without Screen Time authorization.

How do I enable Attention Trigger?

Open Set Up Attention Trigger on Today. Review the explanation, explicitly allow individual Screen Time access, select exactly one application, review your selection, and choose Enable.

Selecting an application alone does not enable its Shield. You can change the selected application or turn the trigger off through its management screen.

What does Continue do?

On the Shield, Continue removes ATENVA’s restriction so you can access the selected application.

In a Shield-sourced Attention Gate, Continue Original releases ATENVA’s selected Shield. In a manual Attention Pause, it simply ends the pause.

ATENVA does not automatically re-enable the Shield after Continue, intercept every launch, or automatically return you to the original application from the Gate.

How do I revoke Screen Time access?

You can turn Attention Trigger off inside ATENVA. To revoke the system authorization, use the Screen Time app-access controls in iOS Settings. Their location and wording can vary by iOS version.

Why did a Helpful App not open?

Helpful Apps use HTTPS Universal Links. Opening can fail if the destination application is unavailable or does not support the link. Use the retry or manual fallback offered by ATENVA.

Launching an application does not automatically complete an action.

Where is my information stored?

Goals, actions, Saved Content, and interaction history are stored locally. Recommendations and Weekly Review are calculated on your device. The current app has no ATENVA account or cloud synchronization.

Opening an external destination or sharing an export uses the destination you choose and its privacy practices.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open Settings → Privacy & Data.

Export creates a JSON copy for the iOS share sheet. ATENVA attempts to clean up its temporary copy after the sheet closes, including after cancellation.

Delete All Local Data removes local product records, pending handoffs, ATENVA’s Screen Time configuration and restrictions, scheduled reminders, and managed temporary exports. Built-in Content and system permissions remain.

Deletion cannot remove copies saved elsewhere or previously created backups. If an operation reports an error, retry it.
