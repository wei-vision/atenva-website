Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026
Publisher: Marat Khusyainov

ATENVA helps you pause before a distracting activity and choose a useful next step. This policy describes the current iOS app.

Information stored on your device

ATENVA stores your Goals and reasons, priorities, Useful Actions, Helpful App settings and links, Saved Content links and titles, optional duration estimates, and interaction history. History includes recommendations, choices, action sessions, completion or dismissal outcomes, and timestamps.

Product records are stored locally in SQLite. Recommendations and Weekly Review are processed on your device. The app and its extensions use a local shared App Group container to exchange Saved Content and pending Shield triggers.

The current app has no ATENVA account, cloud synchronization, AI service, email delivery, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not upload these local records to an ATENVA backend.

Optional Screen Time access

ATENVA requests individual Family Controls authorization only after you choose to allow it. Apple provides the permission prompt and application picker.

ATENVA stores an opaque selection token, authorization-history flag, and your enable preference locally. These support a Shield for the application you select and explicitly enable. ATENVA does not use this permission to read Screen Time usage reports, browsing history, messages, or an inventory of installed applications.

You can turn Attention Trigger off in ATENVA or revoke authorization through iOS Settings. Manual Attention Pauses and other local features remain available without Screen Time access.

Saved Content, Helpful Apps, and external destinations

The Share Extension queues the HTTPS link you choose, available title and host information, a timestamp, and a handoff identifier. It does not fetch webpage metadata. The main app imports or deduplicates queued items; unsuccessful imports remain pending for retry.

Opening Saved Content or a Helpful App passes its destination URL to iOS. The browser, app, or service you open may receive that URL and use its own network services, accounts, and privacy practices. Opening an app is not recorded as proof of completion or measured time spent there.

Notifications and diagnostics

Optional Weekly Review reminders are local iOS notifications with generic text. ATENVA does not implement remote push notifications.

Limited system logging may record a Shield handoff error type without the selected app token, shared URL, or content.

Export and temporary files

Privacy & Data lets you export a JSON copy using the standard iOS share sheet. It includes local product records and a separately read snapshot of pending Saved Content handoffs. Screen Time selection configuration and system permissions are excluded.

ATENVA keeps its temporary export copy available while the share sheet is open, then attempts to remove it when the sheet closes, including after cancellation. It also attempts cleanup of abandoned managed exports on startup and before a new export. Cleanup failures are reported and can be retried.

Any copy you save or share elsewhere is outside ATENVA’s managed temporary storage.

Retention and deletion

History and unsuccessful handoffs have no automatic expiry. Individually deleted Goals or actions may leave historical references until reset.

Delete All Local Data removes product user records, pending shares and Shield triggers, ATENVA-owned restrictions and saved Screen Time configuration, scheduled Weekly Review reminders, and managed temporary exports. Built-in curated Content remains. System permissions are not revoked.

If reset fails, an error is shown and you can retry. Some cleanup may already have occurred. Reset performs logical deletion, not guaranteed forensic erasure.

ATENVA cannot remove copies saved or shared elsewhere, information held by external services, or previously created backups. Device backup settings may affect whether local app data is backed up. Deleting current data does not erase an earlier backup.
